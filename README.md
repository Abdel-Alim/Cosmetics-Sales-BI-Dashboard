# 💄 Cosmetics Sales & Business Intelligence Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI Business Intelligence Dashboard** designed to analyze cosmetics sales performance across **time, products, markets, customers, and detailed transactions**.

The project transforms sales data into a clear, interactive reporting experience that helps users monitor KPIs, identify trends, compare performance, and explore business insights through dynamic filters.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard is built around a consistent **dark + pink cosmetics-inspired theme**, with interactive navigation, KPI cards, analytical visuals, slicers, and detailed summary tables.

### Main Dashboard Pages

- 🏠 **Overview** — High-level business performance and key KPIs
- 📈 **Analysis** — Analytical view of sales and profitability performance
- 🛍️ **Sales** — Sales trends, categories, markets, and profitability
- 📦 **Product** — Product, subcategory, and top-product performance
- 🌍 **Market** — Market, region, and geographic performance
- ⏰ **Time** — Year, month, and day-of-week performance
- 👤 **Customer** — Customer and segment performance
- 📋 **Details** — Detailed transactional/business-level analysis
